# Headline evidence (aggregates from 9,657 answered VANI calls, 5,000 sellers)

- Meeting rate 11.2%; 46% of answered calls end within 20 s (those book 0.04%).
- Meeting rate by situation: asked about the visit 62.6% · asked about price 19.6% · busy 5.0% ·
  not interested 3.8% · confused 2.6%.
- Sellers accept meetings at 11-12 AM and 2-5 PM, mostly "tomorrow" or Monday (1,078 meeting summaries).
  Calls placed at 11 AM convert worst (6.1%), at 3 PM best (15.1%).
- 99% of transcribed sellers answer in (mostly Roman) Hinglish; "who is this?" comes up in 6.2% of calls;
  manager / human requests in 0.9% (none converted when ignored).
- Seller speech: median 2.6 words/s across 279 sellers with timed turns.
- 140 findings in `demo/evidence.json` (rates, counts, 95% CI, p-values, strength), mined with two-proportion
  z-tests, Wilson intervals and a Bonferroni correction. No customer rows.
